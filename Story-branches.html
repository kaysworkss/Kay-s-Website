Your stories

Each story has its own parts, storylines and characters, saved to your account on every device. Use one story per chapter, book or project.

Story account

Use the same email and password on every device. Your story is private to your Firebase account.

Each color is a storyline. Write unconnected ideas with Free node, then choose Attach to storyline. Insert before/after keeps the current storyline. Branch lets you resume an earlier coloured storyline after shared parts, insert a part, or start a new branch. Preview & export lets you select versions. Tap Open for the full document editor. Tap Link, then another box, to share one part between storylines. Drag a box by its title. Parts flow from top to bottom, and Tidy layout re-flows the whole map. Use Characters to give speakers a short code, like A: for Ade, and exports will show the full name. Tap Storyline to edit all the parts of one storyline in a single window.
